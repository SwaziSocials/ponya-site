What is Ponya?

A minute and a half inside a Ponya Pod. Turn your sound on.

Inside a Pod

Close your eyes. When was the last time an hour was only yours?

Every Ponya Pod follows the same slow rhythm, so you always know what is coming and can simply let go.

First, you arrive

Tea in hand. Phone down.

You are welcomed in, handed a warm cup of tea from our herb bar, and invited to set your phone aside. The lights are low and the music is slow.

Then, the soak

A warm ionic foot soak.

Your feet settle into warm water with Celtic salt and our ionic array. It is simply a quiet, warm place to rest while your shoulders drop.

And then, someone asks

Someone really asks.

Your Ponya practitioner sits with you and asks how you are, and waits for the answer. One thing on your mind, held with care. If something comes up, you leave with a clear next step.

Where we begin

Every Pod starts with three questions.

Nourish

Are you nourished?

Food, water and rest that fit your life and your care plan.

Cleanse

Are you cleansing?

Gentle self-care routines that feel good to come back to.

Circulate

Are you circulating?

Warmth, movement and calm that keep you going.

Questions
Good to know.

      

        
Is Ponya medical care?
No. Ponya offers wellness experiences and care navigation support. We don't diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and the foot soak is for comfort and relaxation. We help you stay organized, prepared and cared for while your doctors handle your treatment.

        
Who is Ponya for?
Ponya is for the whole community: anyone who wants an hour of care, families going through a hard season, and the people caring for them. Some programs, like Ponya Circles, are made just for women.

        
Where do Pods happen?
In your home in the Los Angeles area, at our community gatherings, or virtually with your own at-home set. Care navigation check-ins happen by phone or video.

        
What is an ionic foot soak?
A warm foot soak in a basin of water with Celtic salt and a small ionic array. At Ponya it is a calm, warm way to rest during your Pod. We don't make health claims about it.

        
Can someone else pay for my membership?
Yes. Family and friends can gift any tier, and partner organizations may sponsor memberships. Tell us who supports you when you book.

        
When does Ponya open?
We open to members in January and February 2027. Book now to hold a founding spot and founding price.
